**National Research Foundation of Korea** (Grant No. 2021R1F1A1054806; PI: Kyunga Kim) · Biomedical Statistics Center, Samsung Medical Center · Jun. 2021 – Feb. 2023

Developed and compared machine learning-based propensity-score matching methods for imbalanced, three-treatment-group settings, and evaluated performance using both simulation studies and real-world datasets. This work formed the basis of my doctoral research on causal inference in observational studies.

**Tools:** R
