I'm a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Medicine, Division of Gastroenterology & Hepatology at NYU Grossman School of Medicine (NYUGSoM), where my research focuses on applying data science, machine learning, and simulation modeling to clinical and population-level questions in gastrointestinal diseases and cancer.

With more than eight years of experience in biomedical statistics and data science, I develop and apply statistical and machine learning methods to complex healthcare data. Before joining NYU, I served as a Team Leader at the AI Research Center at Samsung Medical Center and as a Research Professor at Sungkyunkwan University in South Korea.

My research spans biomedical statistics, causal inference, predictive modeling, and healthcare AI. My doctoral research focused on developing machine learning-based propensity score methods for causal inference in observational studies, with extensive evaluation through simulation studies. I have also worked with electronic health records, national administrative claims databases, and clinical trial data across a broad range of collaborative clinical research projects at Samsung Medical Center.

My broader research interests center on developing and applying rigorous quantitative methods to generate clinically meaningful evidence from complex healthcare data.
